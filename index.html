    AvenuesEnterprise Platform

    Avenues, powered by Franklin Street, is our enterprise client platform. It shows every target company, who decides there, and who at Franklin Street can reach them, so the right team walks into the first meeting.

    A silent run-through of Avenues: the Command Center, the Company Universe, and one company's six pursuit views.

    One company, six views
    1. Snapshot: what the satellite knows, with a source for every fact.
    2. Portfolio: owned and occupied assets, acquisitions and dispositions.
    3. Relationships: decision-makers and the Franklin Street agents who reach them.
    4. Entry strategy: each service line ranked by our warmest path to the decider.
    5. Account plan: the client journey from Tracked to Pipeline Established.
    6. Opportunities: the service wheel lit up after the discovery meeting.

    PIXELMarketing Platform

    Franklin Street's centralized marketing request system. Submit a request once, then follow it from Open to In Progress to Complete, with every message and file in one place.

    A silent run-through of the live system: the admin dashboard, a ticket, and credit reporting.

    Why PIXEL

    One front door for every marketing request. Ask once, then watch it move to done.

    1. Everything up frontThe intake form captures every project detail, so there is less back-and-forth.
    2. One central queueEach request routes straight to the right Marketing Specialist.
    3. Urgency and due datesRequesters set the priority and the date they need it.
    4. Status at every stageAnyone can see where a request stands, any time.
    5. One conversationUpdates, files and feedback stay with the assigned team member.

    FLOWShared Services Platform

    FLOW runs the operational side of a deal. Requests are routed by team, every deal moves through defined stages, and any team member can pick up a colleague's work because the deal state lives in one shared record.

    A silent run-through of the FLOW prototype with sample deals: tickets, a deal, the AI review queue, coverage, reports and the broker status page.

    Why FLOW

    One shared record for every deal, so the work keeps moving no matter who is in the office.

    1. Start with a text or emailA broker texts or emails to start a deal, and FLOW opens it and routes it to the right team.
    2. Coverage built inIf one person is out, another picks up the deal with full context.
    3. Broker status pageA private, read-only page for each broker, with email and text updates.
    4. Automation built inFLOW creates the SharePoint deal folder and ClickUp task, sends the "received" reply and runs a daily deal check on every deal.

    DEALWISEAI Underwriting

    DEALWISE is our AI-assisted underwriting app. It turns deal documents into a first-pass model faster, so analysts spend their time checking assumptions instead of keying numbers. Select a step.

    Research AutomationGrokbot & AI Agents

    Grokbot gives brokers situational awareness before a pursuit starts: researched target lists, daily trigger searches that give a reason to meet, and org maps of real estate decision-makers. Behind it, a chain of AI agents collects and cleans the data.
